# Row Badges

A badge is a small label after a row's text that shows a [row attribute](row-attributes.md). For example, `@priority : 1` shows as `P1`. Click a badge for a menu.

## Custom & Catch-All Badges

Extensions draw custom badges for the attributes they know, like those in [Tasks & More](tasks-and-more.md). Any other attribute gets a catch-all badge showing its name and value.

## Hiding Unwanted Catch-All Badges

* In Bike > Settings > Extensions > **Attributes**, uncheck the attribute's **Badge** column.

The attribute stays, and you can still edit it in the [Attributes Editor](row-attributes.md#attributes-editor).
