# Using Sidebar

The sidebar shows your home location, pinned rows, an index of headings, and saved filters, each one click away.

## To show or hide the sidebar

* View > Show Sidebar (<kbd>Control-Command-S</kbd>)

## Home

Click Home to unfocus and return to the top of your outline.

## Pinned Items

### To pin or unpin a row

* Outline > Pin Row (<kbd>Command-Y</kbd>)
* Or right-click a pinned item and choose Unpin

## Index

The index lists your headings. Click one to focus it.

## Query Shortcuts

Query shortcuts are saved [filters](outline-filtering.md). With a filter active, right-click the filter field and choose **Save in Sidebar**. Click the shortcut to apply the filter again.

## Focusing an Item

Clicking a sidebar row focuses it. <kbd>Command</kbd>-click to focus its parent and select the row instead, so you see it among its siblings.

## Hiding and Reordering Sections

Use the [Interface Explorer](interface-explorer.md) to hide and reorder the sidebar's items and sections.
