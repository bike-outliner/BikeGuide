# Using Sidebar

Bike's sidebar gives you quick access to navigate your outline's structure. It shows your home location, pinned items, an index of headings, and query shortcuts.

#### To show the sidebar

* View > Show Sidebar (<kbd>Control-Command-S</kbd>)

#### To hide the sidebar

* View > Hide Sidebar (<kbd>Control-Command-S</kbd>)

### Home

The top of the sidebar shows your home location. Click it to unfocus and return to the top of your outline.

### Pinned Items

Pin important rows to the sidebar for quick access. Pinned items stay visible regardless of where you are focused in the outline.

#### To pin a row

* Select the row and use Go > Pin (<kbd>Command-Y</kbd>)

#### To unpin a row

* Select the pinned row and use Go > Unpin (<kbd>Command-Y</kbd>)

### Index

The index section shows headings in your outline by default. Click any heading to focus into that section of your outline.

### Query Shortcuts

Query shortcuts are saved [outline path](using-outline-paths.md) filters. They let you create reusable filtered views of your outline that are accessible from the sidebar.
