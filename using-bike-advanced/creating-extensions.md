# Creating Extensions

Extend and customize Bike with extensions. They introduce new commands, keybindings, views, styles, and more. Sensitive features are safeguarded by a permission system. AI assistants are good at writing them.

The SDK, examples, and reference live in the extension-kit:

- [Bike Extension Kit](https://github.com/bike-outliner/extension-kit)
