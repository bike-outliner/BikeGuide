# What's New?

Bike 2 is a big release. Here are the new features I'm most excited about, with links to learn more.

### Open file formats

Your outlines stay yours, in plain files you can read anywhere.

- **[Bike Markdown](using-bike/bike-markdown-format.md)** — Bike reads and writes plain `.md` files, storing your outline as a subset of standard markdown. Open one in any markdown viewer and it reads just fine.

### Finding your way around

- **[Outline filtering](using-bike/using-outline-filtering.md)** — hide the rows that don't match, so you can focus on just the rows you need.
- **[Navigation sidebar](using-bike/using-sidebar.md)** — move around your outline's structure at a glance. You can also pin rows and save filter queries to the sidebar for quick access.
- **[Calendar](using-bike/using-calendar.md)** — a calendar that lives in the inspector for working with dated rows.

### Making Bike your own

- **[Themes and styles](using-bike/using-themes.md)** — customize Bike's colors and pick separate themes for light and dark mode.
- **[Custom keybindings](using-bike/using-commands.md)** — rebind almost any command through the Commands Explorer.
- **[Minimal full window mode](using-bike/using-windows.md#full-window-mode)** — strip the window down to just your outline when you want to disappear into your writing.
- **[Interface Explorer](using-bike/using-interface-explorer.md)** — organize the views and commands extensions add, deciding how they show up across the window title bar, editor toolbar, status bar, sidebar, and inspector.

### Automating and extending

- **[Extension system](using-bike/using-extensions.md)** — add new commands, keybindings, views, and styles. Sensitive features are guarded by a permission system.
- **[Command line tool](using-bike-advanced/command-line-tool.md)** — the `bike` command lets you open, read, and edit outlines from the terminal and from scripts.
- **[MCP server](using-bike/using-mcp-server.md)** — Bike can run as an MCP server, letting AI assistants read and edit your outlines.
- **[Inspector](using-bike/using-inspector.md)** — an optional panel on the right side of the window that extensions use to show custom tools.

### Smaller touches

- A JSON file format, handy when you want to process your outline with other tools.
- Improved animations throughout.
